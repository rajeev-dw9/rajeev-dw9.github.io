Abstract—I am a PhD student in the Data Science and Engineering Department at IISER Bhopal, where I work with Dr. Vinod Kr. Kurmi in VisDom Lab. My PhD is funded by the TCS Research Scholar Program. I study why vision models learn shortcuts, such as a waterbird recognised by its water rather than its beak, and how to make them work for the groups those shortcuts leave behind. Recent work appeared at CVPR 2026 and ICLR 2026. Most of it is some version of Eq. (1): train a model so the group it serves worst is still served well.

min𝜃 max𝑔∈𝒢 𝔼(𝑥,𝑦)∼𝑃𝑔[ ℓ(𝑓𝜃(𝑥), 𝑦) ] (1)

I came to machine learning from statistics: an Integrated M.Sc. at Central University of Rajasthan and a thesis on heavy-tailed distributions. Dr. Sarika Jain at NIT Kurukshetra introduced me to NLP and the Semantic Web. This summer I was a PhD Tech Intern at Adobe Research, and before that I interned at Sony AI.

Index Terms—fairness in machine learning, spurious correlations, domain adaptation, uncertainty estimation, knowledge graphs.
